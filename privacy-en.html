Privacy Policy

Türkçe sürüm

Jeton collects no data and shares no data.

The app has no internet access permission. The games you play, your scores and your settings stay on your own phone. The developer has no access to any of it, and cannot have.

Personal data collected

None. Jeton never asks for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal information. There is no account and no sign-in. The app contains no component that collects usage statistics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

The technical guarantee behind this is simple: the app never requests Android’s INTERNET permission. Without it, the operating system prevents an app from sending any data over the network.

What is stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone:

  • your score history — the scores you set in each game and when,
  • games in progress — so you can carry on where you left off,
  • your settings — theme, sound and vibration preferences.

All of it lives in the app’s own private storage on your device. No other app can read it, and none of it leaves the device.

Backup

If Android’s backup feature is enabled on your phone, the operating system may back up those three things (scores, games in progress, settings) to your own Google account, so that your scores travel with you to a new phone.

This backup happens through Google’s infrastructure, in your account and under your control; the developer cannot access it. You can turn backup off entirely in your phone’s settings. Google’s handling of this data falls under the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Jeton requests a single permission: vibration. It is used only for in-game haptic feedback while the vibration option in settings is on, and it reads no information. No camera, microphone, location, contacts, file or internet permissions are requested.

Advertising and third parties

The app contains no advertising. It uses no third-party software development kit (SDK) that collects data — no ad network, no analytics service, no crash reporting service, no social media component. There are no in-app purchases.

Children

Jeton is intended for a general audience and can be played by players of any age. Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Deleting your data

You can delete your scores and saved games from the Settings page inside the app. Uninstalling the app also removes everything kept on the device. To remove a Google backup taken earlier, use your phone’s backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that collects data is ever added to the app, this page will be updated and the date noted below. Changes apply going forward only.
