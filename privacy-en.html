Privacy Policy

Türkçe sürüm

Jeton doesn’t know who you are, and doesn’t want to.

There’s no account to create and no advertising. Your games and scores stay on your phone. The little we collect to make the app better is listed below. All of it is anonymous, and usage statistics and announcements are only collected with your permission.

What we collect

  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, we receive the error and technical details such as your phone’s model and Android version, so we can fix it. You can turn this off in Settings. Details
  • Usage statistics — only with your permission. Which games are played, how they go and how long the app is used, so we know what to improve. The app asks once; you can change your answer in Settings. Details
  • Announcements — only with your permission. So we can tell you about a new game or an important update. You can turn this off in Settings. Details
  • App settings. When it starts, the app picks up a few settings from us: whether your version needs updating, whether a game with a problem should be set aside for a while, and which of a few small variations works better, such as the order of games on the front page. These never affect your scores. Details

All of this is anonymous: none of it includes your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or anything else that identifies you. Your phone is told apart only by a random number that isn’t linked to you. Your advertising ID is never read.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, games in progress and settings stay on your phone only. Reminders about a game you left halfway are put together entirely on your phone too; nothing is sent anywhere for them.

We don’t share your information

We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising. Google provides these services for us, and the information may be kept on Google’s servers outside your country.

How long it’s kept

Usage statistics are kept for 2 months and crash reports for 90 days, then deleted.

Permissions

  • Vibration — for haptic feedback in games.
  • Notifications — for announcements and reminders; only asked for after you say yes.
  • Internet — to send and receive the information above.

Backup

If backup is on, your scores and settings may be backed up to your own Google account, so they come with you to a new phone. We can’t access this backup.

Children

Jeton is made for a general audience and is not directed at children.

Deleting your information

Erase all data in Settings deletes everything on your phone and breaks the link between your phone and what we hold. Uninstalling the app also removes everything on the phone. To see your information, ask for it to be deleted, or for any other request (including your rights under GDPR and Turkey’s KVKK), write to us.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we’ll update this page and the date below.
