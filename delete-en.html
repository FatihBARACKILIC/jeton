Deleting your data

Türkçe sürüm · Privacy Policy

You can delete your data in Jeton from inside the app, in one step.

Jeton has no accounts, so deleting is done straight from your phone rather than from an account.

How to delete

  1. Open Jeton.
  2. Go to Settings with the button at the bottom right of the front page.
  3. Tap Erase all data at the bottom of the page.
  4. In the question that opens, tap ERASE IT ALL.

Uninstalling the app also removes everything on the phone; for the Google backup, see below.

Deleted at once

  • Your scores, games in progress and settings.
  • Crash reports not yet sent.
  • The random number that tells your phone apart, and the message number used for announcements.
  • The usage statistics number is renewed, so nothing sent afterwards can be matched to what came before.

Kept for a while

What was already sent can no longer be matched to your phone, and is deleted on its own:

  • usage statistics after 2 months,
  • crash reports after 90 days.

If you want your statistics deleted right away

  1. In Settings → Usage statistics, tap Your player number to copy it.
  2. Send it to fatihb469@gmail.com; we delete your usage statistics within 30 days.
  3. Then tap Erase all data if you like. Erasing renews the number, so send it first.

Without your number we cannot tell which information is yours, since we don’t know who you are; in that case erasing from the app is the quickest and surest way. Crash reports cannot be deleted per person; they contain nothing about you and are deleted on their own after 90 days.

Google backup

If backup is on, your scores, games in progress and settings are backed up to your own Google account; we cannot access that backup. After Erase all data, the next backup empties it too. Uninstalling the app does not delete the backup, and everything comes back when you install Jeton again. To delete the backup right away, use your phone’s backup settings or the Backups section in Google Drive.
