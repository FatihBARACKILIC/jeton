The details

Türkçe sürüm · Privacy Policy

The full list behind the privacy policy.

Nothing that isn’t written here is collected. All of it is anonymous: your phone is told apart only by a random number that isn’t linked to you.

Crash reports

On; you can turn them off in Settings → Crash reports, which also deletes reports waiting to be sent. A report is sent only when the app crashes, and it contains:

  • the error itself — which part of the app it happened in,
  • your phone’s maker, model and Android version,
  • its language and time zone,
  • the connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile data) and the country of the SIM operator,
  • the app version,
  • the random number.

Nothing about your games, your scores or you is added to a report. Kept for 90 days.

Usage statistics

Only if you say yes. The app asks once; you can change it in Settings → Usage statistics. With a yes, these are recorded:

  • when you start a game: which game,
  • when a game ends: which game, which setup (e.g. 4x4 or Easy) and its score,
  • your answer to the notification question: yes or no,
  • the moment Google Play is asked to show its rating card,
  • with announcements on, an announcement arriving and being opened,
  • what is recorded on its own: the app’s first open, the start and length of each use, the app or Android being updated, data being erased and the app being uninstalled.

Each record carries the random number, the app version, phone model, Android version, the phone’s language and the country the connection comes from. The advertising ID is not read, and nothing is used for advertising. Kept for 2 months.

App settings

For everyone. At launch, at most once an hour, the app picks up a few settings from us:

  • whether your version needs updating — to stop an old version if a serious bug turns up,
  • whether a game should be taken off the front page for a while — if a game has a problem, until it is fixed; your scores and your game in progress stay,
  • the values for the tests below.

The request carries the random number, the app version, the Android version, the phone’s language and region setting, and its time zone.

Tests (A/B testing)

To see which version of a few small things works better, players may be put into random groups, and these things may differ by group:

  • the order of the games on the front page,
  • after how many games the notification question is asked,
  • after how many games the rating card is asked for,
  • how many days before a reminder comes,
  • which board or difficulty a player opening a game for the first time starts on — always one you could choose yourself.

The rules of the games and how they are scored never change; scores are compared on the same terms for everyone. Results are counted only from players who allowed usage statistics; if you didn’t, you may still be in a group, but nothing about you is counted.

Announcements

Only if you say yes. The app asks once, after a few finished games. After that, announcements and reminders each have their own switch in your phone’s notification settings; Settings → Notifications takes you there. Until then your phone does not sign up with the announcement service at all.

  • With a yes, your phone gets a random message number and joins the announcement list for its language (Turkish or English).
  • Announcements are written by hand and sent rarely: a new game, an update worth knowing about.
  • Turning announcements off in your phone’s settings deletes the message number the next time the app is opened; Erase all data deletes it at once.

Never collected

Your name, email, phone number, account, location, contacts, photos and files; the advertising ID; the Android ID; the other apps on your phone.
